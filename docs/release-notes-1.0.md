# Desk Bot 1.0 (draft)

A first public release of Desk Bot, a little unofficial desk companion for Grok Bot. Free, from The Wood Lab. Unofficial, not affiliated with xAI, Grok, or Cursor.

## What’s new

This is the first release.

- A small floating orb for the Mac desktop, with eyes that look around, blink, and doze off when things are quiet.
- Click the orb to open the Grok Bot app, a pasted bot link, or nothing, your choice.
- Use the default orb, or drop in your own PNG (cropped to a circle).
- Stay on Top, Launch at login, and Lively or Chill personality, all off or Lively by default as noted in Preferences.
- Hide for an hour from the right-click menu.

## Requirements

macOS 14 or later. Universal (Apple silicon and Intel).

## Privacy, in one line

Coarse activity timing only. No keystroke contents. Nothing leaves the Mac unless you click a link yourself.
